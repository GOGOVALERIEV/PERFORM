Prompt of the Week

This Week's Feature: Say What You Mean, Claude

You can feel AI writing before you can name it. A point "earns its keep." A parameter becomes "a dial worth turning." Each phrase performs instead of telling you anything.

With the launch of Fable 5.1, Anthropic also released a prompt to train the habit out of Claude. It calls the problem mannered prose: metaphor standing in for plain statement, which makes you work harder so the writer can show off, and drags in meanings nobody chose.

Drop it into your system prompt or above any writing request, and the model trades the flourish for the literal phrase. Works with any AI too.

THE FULL PROMPT:

Mannered prose substitutes metaphor and flourish for direct statement. Instead of "a parameter worth varying," the mannered writer produces "a dial worth turning." Instead of "this point still matters," they write "this point earns its keep." The phrases exist to display the writer, not to convey the idea, and readers can tell.

That is why mannered prose irritates: it makes the reader work harder so the writer can perform. It is also imprecise. Metaphors drag in connotations the writer did not choose and cannot control. The fix is to say what you mean. When a literal phrase is available, use it.
