Top AI Humanizers in 2025 – We Tested 6 Tools (Full Comparison)

By Hardik Savani • June 9, 2025
Guest Post

By 2025 AI detectors like GPTZero, Turnitin and Originality AI flag nearly half of machine-generated text, making humanizers essential for turning robotic drafts into smooth, natural prose that reads authentic and evades detection. The six tools below always include BypassGPT and Humbot and cover one-click workflows, bulk API integration, academic modes and live detector feedback.

1. BypassGPT

BypassGPT rewrites AI outputs from ChatGPT, Bard and other generators into fully human-score text in seconds.

Key Features

  • Neural-network rewriting that bypasses GPTZero and Turnitin

  • Built-in SEO booster preserves keyword rankings

  • API and bulk-mode support for large-scale integration

Pricing

  • Free 300 words per month

  • Starter 8 USD per month for 5 000 words

  • Pro 30 USD per month for unlimited use

2. Humbot

Humbot uses a multi-billion-parameter language model to humanize AI text and bypass top detectors while preserving meaning and SEO value.

Key Features

  • One-click humanization with mobile and browser extension support

  • Unlimited free use with no login required

3. QuillBot

QuillBot AI Humanizer refines rigid AI drafts into engaging human-like language with minimal manual edits.

Key Features

  • Chrome and Word integrations for in-document rewriting

  • Built-in grammar, style and plagiarism checks alongside the humanizer

Pricing

  • Free unlimited humanization

  • Premium 9.95 USD per month for advanced modes and higher character limits

4. Phrasly AI

Phrasly AI combines a humanizer with a 99.8 percent accurate detector to ensure zero AI flags on Turnitin, GPTZero and other scanners.

Key Features

  • Multiple paraphrasing modes trained on hundreds of thousands of human articles

  • One-click export to Google Docs and Word formats

Pricing

  • Free 550 words per run

  • Annual plans offer up to 60 percent savings

5. uPass AI

uPass AI targets academic users with three rewrite intensities—Basic, Advanced and Aggressive—and integrates multi-detector checks for Turnitin, GPTZero, Copyleaks and Sapling.

Key Features

  • Essay mode tailored for academic tone and citation formatting

  • Revision history export for version tracking

Pricing

  • Free 80 words per month

  • Custom academic plans up to 30 000 words per month with full history export

6. Uncheck AI

Uncheck AI merges real-time detector scoring (GPTZero, Copyleaks, ZeroGPT) with one-click humanization that outputs polished prose in over 30 languages.

Key Features

  • Live score panel shows AI-score drop instantly during rewriting

  • Enterprise API for automated bulk workflows

Pricing

  • Free trial 100 words

  • Team subscriptions available monthly or yearly

How to Choose

  • For large-scale API integration pick BypassGPT or Uncheck AI

  • For instant no-login ease choose Humbot

  • For academic papers use uPass AI Essay mode

  • For combined detection and rewriting workflow go with Phrasly AI

With any of these six tools you can instantly transform AI-generated drafts into authentic, natural-flowing content without manual rewrites.
