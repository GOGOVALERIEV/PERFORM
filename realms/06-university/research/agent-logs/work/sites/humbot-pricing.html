Frequently Asked Questions

What are the differences between the free and paid plans?

The paid plans come with more word usage and input limit (the number of words you can humanize each time). The output quality is the same and both produce totally undetectable AI writing. So you can try the Free plan to test out our features before subscribing.

What happens if I exceed my plan's limits?

If you exceed your plan's limits, you will be notified and given the option to upgrade your plan

Can I change my plan at any time?

Yes, you can upgrade your plan at any time through your account settings. The new pricing will take effect from your next billing cycle.

Will my payment information be secure?

Absolutely. We use state-of-the-art encryption and follow strict payment information handling policies to ensure the security of your personal information.
