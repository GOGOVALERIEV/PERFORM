What is an AI detector?

An AI detector (also called an AI checker) analyzes text and estimates how likely it was written by an AI model such as ChatGPT, GPT-5, Claude, or Gemini. It doesn't "catch" AI the way a plagiarism checker matches sources — it reads the statistical fingerprints of machine writing: word choices, sentence rhythm, structure, and tone.

Every AI detection result is a probability, not a verdict. That's why JustDone pairs every score with sentence-level evidence — so you can see the reasoning, not just the number.

How AI detection works

Predictability

AI tends to choose the most expected next word; human writing is more varied and surprising.

02

Humans mix short and long sentences; AI keeps a suspiciously consistent beat.

03

Repeated templates, mirrored paragraph shapes, and formulaic transitions are classic AI tells.

04

Generic tone

An overly neutral, hedged voice with stock phrases signals machine writing.

05

Semantic consistency

We validate meaning and flow across the whole document, not isolated sentences.

06

Edited-AI traces

Patterns that survive paraphrasers and grammar tools — so "humanized" AI text is still caught.

How accurate are AI detectors?

Less accurate than most of them claim. Independent benchmarks regularly find gaps between marketing numbers and real performance — especially on edited or paraphrased AI text. JustDone publishes its real, independently benchmarked figures: 80% detection accuracy overall, 98% on academic and scientific texts, and a 10.3% error rate. We'd rather you trust an honest number than a perfect-sounding one.

Accuracy improves with longer texts, and document-level results are more reliable than single-sentence calls. Short fragments, heavily formatted writing, and lists are the hardest cases for any detector.

What to do if your work is flagged

A flag — from JustDone, Turnitin, or any detector — is a starting point, not a conviction. Here's the calm, practical playbook:

  • Review the sentence-level highlights to understand exactly which passages triggered detection and why.
  • Rewrite flagged sections in your own voice — or use the AI Humanizer to restore a natural tone — then rescan to confirm.
  • Keep your drafts, notes, and version history. A visible writing process is the strongest evidence of authorship.
  • If you're falsely accused, ask for the specific flagged passages and explain your process — detectors are probabilistic, and institutions know it.

AI detection vs plagiarism checking

They answer different questions. An AI detector asks "was this likely written by a machine?" A plagiarism checker asks "was this copied from somewhere?" Text can be original yet AI-generated, or plagiarized yet fully human-written. For academic work, run both — JustDone includes an AI plagiarism checker alongside the detector, so you can verify originality and authorship in one place.

Using AI responsibly

AI is a legitimate writing aid when your institution or employer allows it — for brainstorming, outlining, or polishing. The line is presenting AI-generated work as entirely your own where disclosure is required. Check your syllabus or company policy, cite AI assistance when rules call for it, and use detection to understand how your writing reads, not to disguise work that isn't yours.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AI detector?

An AI detector is a tool that reads your text and estimates how much of it was likely written by an AI model such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. JustDone returns a score plus sentence-level flags, so you see which lines look machine-written.

 

 

How does an AI detector work?

It analyzes text and estimates whether it was written by a person or by an artificial intelligence model. JustDone uses several detection methods: 

  • Text predictability measures how predictable the words used are. AI tends to choose more expected words, while human writing tends to be more varied and unpredictable. 
  • Sentence uniformity score evaluates whether sentences maintain similar structures. Humans naturally mix short and long sentences, while AI keeps a more consistent rhythm. 
  • Linguistic patterns checker identifies typical signs of AI, such as generic phrases, repetitions, or an overly neutral tone. 

These methods are combined with algorithms trained on large volumes of human and AI-generated texts. Our detector calculates a score indicating the likelihood that the content was created by artificial intelligence. However, no detector is completely accurate and can make errors in both directions.

What can an AI detector detect?

It can detect and analyze diverse content elements, such as images, text, keywords, and more. It can also identify patterns, trends, and anomalies, enabling users to leverage valuable insights for their content creation and optimization strategies.

How accurate is an AI detector?

No detector is perfect. But some tools are more accurate, than others. For instance, JustDone shares real benchmarks: 80% accuracy overall and 98% on academic and scientific texts. Results are most reliable on longer documents.


 

Can an AI detector improve content quality?

Yes. Beyond a score, JustDone flags weak, robotic lines and helps you reword them, so your final draft reads clearer and more like you.

 

Is an AI detector user-friendly for content creators?

Absolutely! The JustDone AI detector is designed to be user-friendly, offering intuitive features and easy-to-use tools that empower content creators to leverage the full potential of artificial intelligence for their content creation and optimization endeavors.

What is the best AI detector?

It depends on what you're checking and how much accuracy you need. A good AI checker should catch text from tools like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini without falsely flagging normal human writing. JustDone's AI Detector is built to do just that — quick scans, clear results, and it holds up well even on text that's been lightly edited after being AI-generated.


 

What AI detector do colleges use?

Most colleges don't rely on just one tool. Some use Turnitin's built-in AI detection since it's bundled with the plagiarism software they already have, while others use standalone checkers like JustDone alongside it for a second opinion. Policies vary a lot by school and even by individual professor, so it's worth checking your syllabus or asking directly if you're unsure.

 

How to check for AI writing?

Just paste the text into an AI detector like JustDone and it'll scan the writing and give you a score showing how likely it is that AI was involved. It usually only takes a few seconds, and you can check anything from a short paragraph to a full essay or article.
