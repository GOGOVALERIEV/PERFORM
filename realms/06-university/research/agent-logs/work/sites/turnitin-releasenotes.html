2026 September 9

Mobile responsiveness for the Turnitin Clarity writing space

Turnitin license(s): Feedback Studio w/ Turnitin Clarity add-on

Students can now fully write and edit within the Turnitin Clarity writing space directly from their phone-sized devices.

With this update, the interface automatically scales and adapts to smaller screens without sacrificing core functionality. Students can type, edit, format, and review their drafts with the same ease on a smartphone as they would on a laptop.

2026 August 18

Hide grader details from students in Standard and Clarity assignments

Turnitin license(s): Feedback Studio, Feedback Studio w/ Turnitin Clarity add-on

Instructors can now hide grader details from students when providing feedback. They can do this using the Hide grader details from students setting in the Grading and Feedback settings section of the Standard and Clarity assignment settings.

When Hide grader details from students is selected, the grader’s name and the timestamp associated with comments and QuickMarks are hidden from students. All feedback remains visible, so students can still review the comments and QuickMarks provided on their submission.

This setting is not available for Standard (Classic) assignments.

2026 August 18

New AI detection writing detection capabilities for Modern Standard Arabic submissions

Turnitin license(s): Originality add-on

English العربية 

Turnitin is excited to release its AI writing detection capabilities in Arabic to help educators uphold academic integrity for Modern Standard Arabic language submissions.

When AI writing is licensed and enabled by administrators, submissions written in Modern Standard Arabic can now be processed for likely AI-generated text. Once submitted, an AI writing report is generated for instructors to review. Any previously submitted assignments can be checked with our AI writing detector when they are re-submitted to Turnitin, and you have AI writing enabled for your account. 

Please note our Arabic AI writing model is a separate model from our English AI writing,  Spanish AI writing, and Japanese AI writing models. Currently, Turnitin’s Arabic AI writing detection model can detect content from GPT-5.2 Pro (released 2025-12), GPT-5.3 Chat (released 2026-02), GPT-5.4 (released 2026-03), Claude Opus 4.5 (released 2025-11) and 4.6 (released 2026-03), Claude Sonnet 4.6 (released 2026-05), Gemini 2.5 Pro (released 2025-06), Gemini 3 Flash (released 2025-12), Gemini 3.1 Pro (released 2026-02), DeepSeek R1 (released 2025-01), DeepSeek V3.1 (released 2025-08) and 3.2 (released 2025-012), Grok 4.1 Fast (released 2025-11), Llama 4 Maverick (released 2025-04), Llama 4 Scout (released 2025-04), and OpenAI o3 (released 2025-04), Mistral Large 3 (released on 2025-12), and Qwen 3 Next (released 2025-09). As our Arabic, Japanese, Spanish, and English AI detection capabilities differ, please read more about how our Arabic AI detection capabilities work. 

The Arabic AI writing report is built into our existing Similarity Report experience. Access the AI writing report from the top menu by selecting the AI Writing tab, which contains the overall percentage of text in the document that is detected as likely AI-generated.

2026 August 6

Multipart assignments

Turnitin license(s): Feedback Studio, Feedback Studio w/ Clarity

Multipart assignments are now available, giving instructors a new way to create connected learning experiences that guide students through drafting, feedback, reflection, and revision across multiple assignment stages.

Instructors can link up to eight Standard and Clarity assignments into a single multipart workflow, creating structured checkpoints that support their teaching goals. Students can review feedback and improve their work before progressing to the next part, while Clarity assignments preserve draft continuity so students can continue writing from where they left off.

Instructors can also navigate between assignment parts to track how student work develops over time, review whether previous feedback has been addressed, and identify where additional support may be needed.

As part of this release, assignment type names have also been updated in the assignment picker. Student Writing is now Clarity, and New Standard is now Standard, and Standard is now Standard (Classic).

2026 August 4

AI paraphrase detection no longer a separate category in the AI Writing Report

Turnitin license(s): Feedback Studio w/ Originality add-on

We’ve updated the AI Writing Report to combine the previous two categories blue and purple into a single blue category. Now, ‘AI-generated text that was AI-paraphrased’ will be combined with the ‘AI-generated only’ detection category, so the report will show a single ‘AI-generated’ category. This means purple highlights previously used for AI-paraphrased text will no longer be shown. 

The model will continue to detect likely AI generated content that may have been further modified by AI paraphrasers or bypassers. We will now only show one set of highlights to identify likely AI-generated content.

The AI Writing Report updates will apply to newly submitted files. To access the latest AI Writing Report on existing submissions, please resubmit the file to generate the updated report.

New EULA workflow

2026 June 17

Audio and video summary comments

Turnitin license(s): Feedback Studio

Instructors can now add an audio or video summary comment in Feedback Studio so students can review overall feedback alongside in-paper comments and QuickMarks.

Audio or video summaries allow instructors to provide personalized overall feedback alongside written summary comments, QuickMarks, and in-paper feedback. Audio and video recordings can be up to five minutes long and are available for students to review when viewing their Similarity Report.

Customize AI chat responses in Clarity assignments

Turnitin license(s): Clarity add-on

Instructors can now customize how the AI chat supports students during the writing process in Clarity assignments. This includes adjustable levels of helpfulness and response complexity, with the ability to preview sample responses before enabling settings.

By default, the AI chat asks questions to help students reflect on their ideas and provides feedback on assignment and rubric alignment. Instructors can also choose to enable additional support options, including planning and brainstorming help, illustrative examples, revision suggestions, and proofreading assistance.

Instructors can additionally select the complexity level of AI responses using one of three options:

  • Standard for standard academic language and sentence structures
  • Advanced for more advanced academic language and discipline-specific conventions

2026 April 30

Assignment creation restricted for LMS-managed classes and login issue resolved

Turnitin license(s): Feedback Studio, Originality Check

We’ve introduced an update to how assignments are created for LMS-integrated classes, alongside a fix for a recent login issue affecting some users.

Assignment creation restricted for LMS-managed classes
For classes originally created through an LMS (such as Canvas, Moodle, or Blackboard), assignments can no longer be added directly from turnitin.com or turnitinuk.com. Assignment creation and management must now be completed within the LMS.

This change helps ensure data accuracy by preventing synchronisation issues between platforms, and improves security by reducing the risk of unauthorised access or data inconsistencies. Assignments created in the LMS will continue to sync automatically with Turnitin for reporting and viewing.

If users want to manage assignments both in the LMS and directly on the Turnitin website, they will need to create a new class within the website and manage it separately. This update only applies to LMS-managed classes. Classes created directly on turnitin.com or turnitinuk.com are not affected.

Resolved login issue
We also resolved an issue that impacted some users when logging into turnitin.com and turnitinuk.com. We apologise for any disruption this may have caused and appreciate your patience while this was addressed.

2026 March 20

Improvements to Clarity's writing space

Turnitin license: Feedback Studio w/ Clarity add-on

  1. Prevent quote blocks within quote blocks
  • Previously, when a student working in Clarity’s composition space selected the Insert block quote button in the toolbar, and then clicked the same button again, a nested quote block was created within the original quote block. 
  • Now, when clicking the Insert Block Quote button multiple times, the block quote is added and then removed, instead of creating nested block quotes. This issue is now resolved and working as intended.
  1. Preserve text formatting on new lines
  • Previously, there was an issue where formatting (such as fonts, font sizes, etc.) was not preserved when authors would create a new line, navigate away from the new line via keyboard or mouse, then return to the newline and start typing. The text formatting would revert to defaults. 
  • Now, the formatting set by the student is preserved on the new line. This issue is resolved and working as intended.
  1. Table formatting issue resolved
  • Previously, tables created in Clarity’s editor were distorted when submitted as PDFs. 
  • Now, this issue is  resolved. Tables will render as expected in student submissions and downloads.
  1. Arabic alignment issue with lists, quotes, and indents resolved
  • Previously when entering text in Arabic, certain formatting elements were aligned incorrectly, including bulleted and numbered lists, block quotes, and hanging indents. 
  • Now, this issue is resolved and working as expected for Arabic text.
  1. Two Improvements to footnote editing
  • When selecting a footnote in the footnote editing area at the bottom of the document, the document now automatically jumps to the corresponding location within the body text.
  • Previously, using the Backspace key in the footnote area would cause multiple footnotes to be selected. This issue is now resolved and working as expected.
